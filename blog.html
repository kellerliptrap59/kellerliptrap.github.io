How Do the Cleveland Cavaliers Perform in Home and Away Wins and Losses?

The question

How do the Cleveland Cavaliers' average points, assists, rebounds, and three-point shooting percentage differ between wins and losses at home and on the road during the 2025–2026 NBA season?

The approach

I used a Common Table Expression (CTE) to isolate Cleveland Cavaliers game records and classify each game as home or away based on the matchup. I then grouped the games by result and location, calculating the average points, assists, rebounds, and three-point shooting percentage for each combination.

I used aggregate functions with GROUP BY to compare performance across the four categories: home wins, home losses, away wins, and away losses. I also rounded the averages to two decimal places to make the results easier to interpret. 

WITH cavs_stats AS (
    SELECT
        team_name,
        matchup,
        TRIM(REGEXP_REPLACE(matchup, 'CLE|vs\.|@', '')) AS opponent,
        CASE
            WHEN matchup LIKE '% vs. %' THEN 'HOME'
            ELSE 'AWAY'
        END AS location,
        wl,
        pts,
        ast,
        reb,
        fg3_pct
    FROM nba_2025_2026_games
    WHERE team_name = 'Cleveland Cavaliers'
)
SELECT
    wl AS result,
    location,
    ROUND(AVG(pts), 2) AS avg_points,
    ROUND(AVG(ast), 2) AS avg_assists,
    ROUND(AVG(reb), 2) AS avg_rebounds,
    ROUND(AVG(fg3_pct), 2) AS avg_3pt_percent
FROM cavs_stats
GROUP BY wl, location
ORDER BY
    FIELD(location, 'HOME', 'AWAY'),
    FIELD(result, 'W', 'L');

The result

The query produces four rows summarizing the Cavaliers' average performance: home wins, home losses, away wins, and away losses. Each row includes points, assists, rebounds, and three-point shooting percentage, allowing direct comparisons between winning and losing performances at different locations.

Takeaway: By combining a CTE, conditional logic, aggregate functions, and grouping, I created a comparison of the Cavaliers' performance across home and away games. This analysis helps identify which statistical differences are associated with wins and losses and whether those patterns change depending on whether the game is played at home or away.

There are several things that stood out to me when looking at this data. First, when the Cavs win, regardless of whether they play at home or away, they average nearly 10 more points than their opponents. Specifically, at home, this may be attributed to their nearly 10 percentage-point increase in three-point shooting percentage in wins compared to home losses.

>Which Teams Did Donovan Mitchell Perform Best Against in the 2025–2026 NBA Regular Season?

The question

How did Donovan Mitchell's scoring, three-point shooting, field-goal shooting, and playing time vary against different opponents during the 2025–2026 NBA regular season?

The approach

I used a Common Table Expression (CTE) to isolate Donovan Mitchell's game-level statistics and identify each opponent from the matchup data. I joined the player box scores with the game records using the game ID and team abbreviation to match each performance to the correct game.

I then used aggregate functions, including AVG() and COUNT(), with GROUP BY to calculate his average performance and number of games against each opponent. Finally, I used ORDER BY to rank opponents by his average points per game. 

with donovan_mitchell_stats as(
SELECT 
    CONCAT(box.firstName, ' ', box.familyName) AS player_name,
    CASE
        WHEN game.MATCHUP LIKE 'CLE %' 
            THEN SUBSTRING_INDEX(game.MATCHUP, ' ', -1)
        ELSE SUBSTRING_INDEX(game.MATCHUP, ' ', 1)
    END AS opponent,
    box.points pts,
    box.threePointersMade threes_made,
    box.threePointersPercentage three_pct,
    box.fieldGoalsMade fgm,
    box.fieldGoalsPercentage fg_made,
    box.minutes min
FROM nba_boxscores AS box
JOIN nba_2025_2026_games AS game
    ON box.gameId = game.game_Id
    AND box.teamTricode = game.TEAM_ABBREVIATION
WHERE box.personId = 1628378
ORDER BY opponent
)
SELECT 
    player_name,
    opponent,
    COUNT(*) AS games_played,
    ROUND(AVG(pts), 2) AS avg_points,
    ROUND(AVG(threes_made), 2) AS avg_threes_made,
    ROUND(AVG(three_pct), 3) AS avg_three_pct,
    ROUND(AVG(fgm), 2) AS avg_fgm,
    ROUND(AVG(fg_made), 2) AS avg_fg_made,
    ROUND(AVG(min), 2) AS avg_minutes
FROM donovan_mitchell_stats
GROUP BY player_name, opponent
ORDER BY avg_points DESC;
